What each check measures

Expand a check to see what causes a failure and how to fix it.

Measures edge clarity across the document. A blurry photo usually means the camera moved while the shutter was open.

Fix Rest your phone or scanner on a surface, or hold steady.

Looks for grain and speckling, which usually come from a dim scene where the camera boosts its gain.

Fix Add more light rather than brightening the image afterwards.

Compares overall brightness and contrast. Very dark or washed-out images fail here first.

Fix Avoid direct flash and shoot in even, indirect light.

Measures how evenly the page is lit. A bright corner with a dark opposite corner is a common cause of rejection.

Fix Use a single light source away from the page.

Detects reflections and hotspots on the surface of the document.

Fix Tilt the document slightly and move away from direct light.

Estimates print resolution. Small images scaled up look soft even when the focus is fine.

Fix Capture at the highest quality the device offers.

Checks whether the document edges look rectangular. Phone photos taken at an angle often skew.

Fix Hold the camera parallel to the page.

Measures how much of the frame the document fills. A small document in a large frame loses detail.

Fix Move closer until the page fills most of the frame.

Checks that no part of the document is cut off by the edge of the image.

Fix Leave a small margin around every edge.

Before you upload

  1. 1 Place the document on a plain, contrasting surface.
  2. 2 Use indirect light and turn off the flash.
  3. 3 Keep the camera parallel to the page.
  4. 4 Fill the frame without cropping any edge.
  5. 5 Hold steady and check the preview before submitting.

Accepted files

  • JPEG
  • PNG
  • WebP
  • Up to 10 MB
